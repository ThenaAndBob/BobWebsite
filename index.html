About Us
Bob's Boosting is a modern gaming service providing fast and reliable boosting for a reasonable price. By providing our boosters with much greater compensation than our competitors we are able to keep your prices low while simultaneously increasing the quality and speed of the service by employing the highest ranked boosters.
Our Experience
While the website is new and will continue to be developed, our boosting has been perfected over years with hundreds of orders. Every person working for Bob's Boosting has years of experience helping gamers reach their goals. However, due to the poor conditions in the industry where owners take up to a 70% cut on all orders, we decided to set up our own business through Discord, and now through this website.

Because all of the owners are also boosters we are able to keep our cut at 20%, far below the industry standard. Therefore providing you with an inexpensive boost without sacrificing quality in any way.
